OUR FOUNDER

Dr. T. Gurusamy

Founder & Chairman, Star Guru Charitable Foundation

Dr. T. Gurusamy founded Star Guru Charitable Foundation with a vision of serving humanity through compassion, dignity, and selfless service. His leadership has inspired thousands of volunteers to participate in food distribution, environmental conservation, educational support, and healthcare initiatives.

Under his guidance, the foundation has served over one million meals, transformed communities, and continues to create a positive social impact across Tamil Nadu.
