Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Have questions about volunteering? Find answers to the most commonly asked questions below.

Who can become a volunteer?

Anyone with a passion for serving the community is welcome. Students, professionals, homemakers, senior citizens, and corporate teams can all volunteer.

Is there any registration fee?

No. Volunteering with Star Guru Charitable Foundation is completely free. There are no registration or membership fees.

Do I need prior experience?

No previous experience is required. We provide guidance and orientation before you begin volunteering.

Can students volunteer?

Yes. Students are encouraged to volunteer and gain valuable leadership, teamwork, and community service experience.

How much time should I commit?

You can volunteer based on your availability. Whether it's a few hours on weekends or regular participation, every contribution makes a difference.

Will I receive a certificate?

Active volunteers may receive a certificate of appreciation recognizing their service and contribution to the community.
